## About me

I'm a first-year master's student in Data Science (Machine Learning & Statistics) at Uppsala University, after a B.Tech in AI & Data Science at Panimalar Engineering College in Chennai. I like finding patterns in messy datasets and building models that (almost) think for themselves. Lately that has mostly meant LLM apps: wiring local models like Mistral 7B into tools people can actually click around in.

I care about AI being explainable and fair as well as accurate, and I try to design that in from the start.
