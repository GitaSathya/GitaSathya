### Projects

**[MarketPulse](https://github.com/GitaSathya/MarketPulse-v2)**: crypto analytics app  
Pulls live market data from Binance, CryptoCompare and CryptoPanic, draws moving-average and RSI charts, scores news sentiment, and answers questions through an LLM chatbot (Mistral 7B running on llama.cpp).  
`Python` `Flask` `LangChain` `llama.cpp` `Plotly`

**[HR-Assist / AI Resume Segregator](https://github.com/GitaSathya/AI-Resume-Segregator)**: recruitment platform  
Covers the full hiring workflow: recruiters post jobs, applicants upload resumes, OCR extracts the text, and semantic similarity ranks candidates against each role for shortlisting.  
`Python` `Streamlit` `NLP` `LLMs` `OCR`

**[Audio Analysis & Chord Recognition](https://github.com/GitaSathya/Chord-Spacelete)**: deep learning on sound  
Takes any WAV file, extracts MFCC, chroma and spectral features, predicts chords with a CNN, and plots the waveform and spectrogram. Built to help deaf and hard-of-hearing people follow music. This became a published paper (below).  
`TensorFlow` `CNN` `Audio`

**[OTT Popularity Prediction](https://github.com/GitaSathya/OTT_Platform_Predictive_Analysis)**: predictive analytics  
Predicts how popular 2023 Netflix titles will be from type, genre, release year and duration, with an interactive Power BI dashboard.  
`Python` `scikit-learn` `Power BI`
